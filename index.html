FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I have to have my own training gear?+

We do have a few pairs of loaner boxing gloves and mitts, but highly recommend that you purchase your own. We have options available for purchase on site.

What is the minimum age limit?+

Our children's classes start at 4 years old, and our adult classes start at 13 years old.

Are classes co-ed?+

Yes, our classes are co-ed but we pride ourselves on an inclusive atmosphere.

I've never tried Mixed Martial Arts, can I still train here?+

Absolutely! Our instructors will make sure you work on the basics and start your journey with good form. Our classes are all levels, giving you an opportunity to work with partners of all levels to help you improve and grow in your training.

Is a uniform required?+

We require a uniform for our children's classes, provided to you by us. Students are encouraged to layer comfortably underneath the uniform. We do not require a dojo specific uniform for the adult classes, however, appropriate fitness attire is preferred. BJJ classes require a gi.
